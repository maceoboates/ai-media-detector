DIGITAL MEDIA GUIDE

Can you tell when AI is fooling you?

Artificial intelligence can now create incredibly realistic photos, videos, and voices. Learn a few simple ways to slow down, look closer, and protect yourself from misinformation and scams.

Learn the warning signs ↓
START HERE

AI doesn't always look like AI.

Modern AI can create media that looks and sounds real. That doesn't mean you need to be an expert to question it. The most important habit is simple: slow down before you believe or share something.

01 — SPOT THE SIGNS

Things worth looking at twice

No single clue proves that something is AI-generated. Look for several unusual details together.

01

Look at hands

AI-generated images can sometimes produce unusual fingers, strange hands, or objects being held in unnatural ways.

02

Examine faces

Look closely at eyes, teeth, ears, glasses and hair. Small details can sometimes look inconsistent.

03

Read the writing

Signs, menus, shirts and advertisements may contain letters or words that look almost correct but don't actually make sense.

04

Check the background

Look for objects that seem distorted, repeated, melted together, or physically impossible.

05

Watch the lighting

Shadows and reflections should make sense. Look for objects whose lighting doesn't match everything around them.

06

Ask where it came from

Consider who posted the media and whether trustworthy sources are reporting the same event.

02 — DIFFERENT TYPES OF MEDIA

AI isn't limited to pictures.

◉

Photos

Zoom in and inspect hands, faces, text, reflections, shadows and background objects.

▶

Videos

Watch for unnatural facial movements, strange lip syncing, flickering details, or objects changing between frames.

♪

Voices

AI-generated voices can sound extremely convincing. Be cautious when someone asks for money or sensitive information unexpectedly.

03 — BEFORE YOU SHARE

Take 30 seconds.

If something makes you surprised, angry, or scared, that's a good reason to pause.

1 Stop

Don't immediately share or respond.

2 Look closer

Inspect the details and ask what seems unusual.

3 Check the source

Find out who originally posted it.

4 Verify

Look for confirmation from reliable sources.
