START HERE

AI doesn't always look like AI.

AI tools can create images, videos, and voices that look remarkably real. You do not need to know how AI works to protect yourself.

The most important habit is simple: pause when something makes you feel surprised, angry, excited, or afraid.

01 — SPOT THE SIGNS

Six clues worth a second look.

No single clue proves something is AI. Look for several unusual details together.

01
✋

Look at hands

Check fingers, thumbs, jewelry, and the way a person is holding an object.

02
◉

Examine faces

Look at eyes, teeth, ears, glasses, hair, and facial expressions.

03
Aa

Read the writing

Signs, menus, shirts, and labels can contain almost-correct words or strange letters.

04
⌁

Check backgrounds

Look for objects that melt together, repeat, bend, or appear physically impossible.

05
☼

Watch the lighting

Shadows and reflections should agree with the objects and light around them.

06
↗

Check the source

Ask who posted it and whether reliable sources are reporting the same event.

02 — IT'S MORE THAN PHOTOS

AI can imitate almost any kind of media.

◉
IMAGE

Photos

Inspect hands, faces, text, reflections, shadows, and background objects.

▶
VIDEO

Videos

Watch for odd lip-syncing, flickering details, unnatural movement, or objects changing between frames.

♪
AUDIO

Voices

Never send money or sensitive information just because a familiar voice asks you to.

04 — THE 30 SECOND RULE

Pause. Check. Then act.

If a message makes you feel an immediate emotional reaction, give yourself 30 seconds before clicking, replying, or sharing.

1

STOP

Don't immediately share or respond.

2

LOOK CLOSER

Inspect the details and ask what seems unusual.

3

CHECK THE SOURCE

Find out who originally posted it.

4

VERIFY

Look for confirmation from reliable sources.

05 — AI SCAMS

A familiar voice isn't always proof.

01

Family emergency: Someone claims to be a relative who urgently needs money.

02

Impersonation: A caller or video appears to be a friend, celebrity, boss, or official.

03

Pressure: You're told to act immediately and keep the request secret.

04

Best response: Hang up and contact the person using a number you already trust.
